Automatically Calculate Time Interval

Will divide the presentation into sections and notify you every section.

HINT: If you are given a 30 minute time slot to present and you have 5 sections in your presentation, 5 would be your section amount. You can also include the amount of group members you have for the section amount to split the presentation time evenly among people.

Formula: [Time Interval] / [Presentation Time] = [Alert Frequency]

Manually Set Time Interval

Will notify you by a set amount of time for the length of the presentation.

HINT: If you are presenting a 7 minute presentation where you know that each group member is allowed 2.5 minutes maximum to present, you can input 2.5 minutes as the time interval, and you will be notified every 2.5 minutes, regardless of the total length.

Formula: [Time Interval] = [Alert Frequency] for [Presentation Time]
